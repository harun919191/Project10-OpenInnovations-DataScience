| Resource | Provenance and treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Fashion-MNIST | [Zalando Research](https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist), Han Xiao, Kashif Rasul and Roland Vollgraf; MIT. Original licence retained at Project2/data/LICENSE_FASHION_MNIST.txt. Working split transforms ordering only. |

| Stroke data | [Vivian Sam / Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/viviansam/cerebral-stroke-dataset). Report cites Mendeley/Kaggle provenance; complete collection provenance and redistribution rights unresolved. Raw and patient-level records excluded. |
| ML libraries | TensorFlow/Keras, PyTorch, NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, imbalanced-learn, XGBoost, SHAP, SciPy, matplotlib, seaborn, joblib and IPython are dependencies, not vendored code. Their own terms govern installation. |

No university slides, briefs, externally supplied teaching notebooks or complete assessment reports are redistributed. Saved plots are generated analytical outputs; Fashion-MNIST images/derived assets remain covered by the upstream notice. No general licence for every repository file is asserted.
